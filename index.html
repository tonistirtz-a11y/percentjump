Percent Logo Bakery: Putting the Logo On

HM Brownies? Frost it, count it, stamp the logo

Percent means brownies. To change a decimal to a percent, ask one question: How many brownies is that?

1. Frost it on the cake.
Tap a brownie to frost up to it. Each column is a Kit Kat (10 brownies).
2. HM brownies is that?
brownies
3. Stick the percent logo on!
Brownie cake (100 brownies = 100%)
0 brownies frosted
Parent tip: Have your child read the decimal out loud by place value (“13 hundredths,” never “point one three”) and say “HM brownies?” before counting.

Jump Machine: 2 jumps bigger, then the logo

Multiplying by 100% makes every digit jump 2 places bigger and sticks the percent logo on. 100% is one whole cake, which is just 1, so the frosting never changes.

Try this: Press Logo on! too early and see what happens. Each jump makes the number 10 times bigger. Two jumps bigger is × 100.
